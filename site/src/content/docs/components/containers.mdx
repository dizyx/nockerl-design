The **planes** content lives on. Every surface either **lifts** off the ground or
**recedes** into it. One consistent light source decides which.

### Lift vs recede

- **Cards lift**: a clear luminance step above a darker ground + drop shadow + top
  catch-light.
- **Panels / wells recede**: darker + inner shadow (the same move that defines a field).
- **Ground is the darkest layer.** Lift comes from the luminance gap, not from making a
  card light-gray (see [Elevation &amp; depth](/foundations/elevation-and-depth/)).

### Elevation ladder

Depth is ordered, not arbitrary. Pick a tier, don't tune a shadow by hand:

- **L1**: resting card / panel on the canvas.
- **L2**: raised card, hover, popover.
- **L3**: sheet, drawer, dialog.
- **L4**: transient overlay (tooltip, menu) above everything.

### Material & shape

- Depth = **neutral (or tinted-black) shadow + top catch-light, never a glow** or colored
  shadow (see [Design laws](/foundations/design-laws/)).
- **Card radius 16**, control radius 12. Surfaces read softer than the controls inside
  them.
