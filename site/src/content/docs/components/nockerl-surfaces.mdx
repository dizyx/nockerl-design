The **originality layer**. Everywhere else the system earns trust by being consistent;
*here* it earns identity. This is where Nockerl stops looking like a dark theme and starts
looking like Nockerl, sourced straight from the **Android app + Voice**.

### The Nockerl feel

- **Future-oriented, dimensional.** Depth is a feature: real shadow, catch-light, and 3D,
  not flat.
- **Rich visuals, sparse text.** Let the surface carry the meaning.
- **Distinctive over popular.** Refuse the trained mean (see the
  [ethos](/foundations/design-laws/)).

### What makes a surface signature

- **Gradient user chat bubbles** (a jewel-like diagonal cyan accent) vs **lifted
  gateway / agent messages** (a raised plane, family-color rail). The sender is legible from
  shape and depth, not just alignment.
- **Faceted / geometric backgrounds**: a low-poly ambient field that adds dimension
  without noise.
- **The recording HUD**: a phase-driven voice pill that reads its own state.
- **Long-press pop**: Android press-and-hold that lifts a surface into a contextual menu
  with haptics.

Build these on the foundations and the [laws](/foundations/design-laws/) (the brand
gradient, family rails, depth, and motion), composed into forms that exist nowhere else.
