## What this is

**Nockerl Design is an open source design framework.** It is MIT licensed and free for
anyone to use, in commercial work as much as in personal projects, and you are welcome
here whether or not you have ever heard of the rest of Nockerl.

It is built first for the Nockerl suite, which includes Nockerl Voice, Nockerl Security
and Nockerl Design itself. That is why it is opinionated rather than a neutral toolkit:
the decisions are already made, written down, and enforced by the build. It is also what
makes it worth borrowing, because a framework that real products ship on has had its
edges found.

Three builds come out of that one source, and they are the same ones those products
install:

- **Android**, as Kotlin and Jetpack Compose, published to Maven Central.
- **macOS**, as a Swift package, installed through Swift Package Manager.
- **Web**, as React, published to npm.

The current version is **{pkg.version}** and the framework is in active development. The
foundations are settled and the products ship on them, but the component surface is still
moving, so read a minor version as something that can change an API. None of that should
put you off. Try it, and the [Installation](/getting-started/installation/) page has what
to add to your build.

These are the products it dresses today. Every mark below is read straight from
`logos/app-icons/` in this repository, the same source the products themselves consume, so
none of them keeps a private copy and a brand change ships everywhere at once.

## How it fits together

<Pipeline />

The pipeline turns one DTCG source of truth into per-platform outputs, publishes them as
versioned packages, and this site documents the result: foundations, the design laws,
and a page per component showing it across every platform with live examples, states,
accessibility, and review status.
